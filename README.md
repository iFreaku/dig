# Signal Lab — Digital Electronics exam preparation

A self-contained, browser-based study website built from the uploaded Digital Electronics internal papers and handwritten class-note scans.

## Run it

1. Extract the ZIP to any folder.
2. Open `index.html` in Firefox, Chrome or Edge.
3. Use the left navigation to open the exam priorities, PYQs, detailed lessons, predicted practice, quiz, revision centre, notes audit and video resources.

No Node.js, Python, server, account or package installation is required.

## Included

- 10 topic modules with explanations, formulas, complete truth tables, worked examples, diagrams, exam pointers and common mistakes.
- All 10 readable questions from the two supplied internal papers, with original paper year, section, numbering and marks.
- Detailed worked answers for every readable PYQ.
- A 12-question expandable predicted-practice bank. Suggested mark formats are clearly labelled as practice formats, not guaranteed marks.
- 15 interactive MCQs with explanations.
- Formula/definition/truth-table revision centre.
- Interactive logic-gate tester and 4:1 MUX selector.
- Search across lessons, PYQs and video resources.
- Local bookmarks and completed-topic tracking using browser storage.
- Notes audit with corrections, formula checks and missing-content list.
- Curated Gate Smashers videos with direct YouTube links.

## Data and privacy

Search, quiz, bookmark and topic-completion state run locally in the browser. There is no login and no remote database. YouTube links require internet access; the rest of the site works offline after extraction.

## Source limitations

The package does not include copies of the original PDFs; they remain in the conversation uploads. The 2025 paper has no visible Q5, and its NAND-only expression is not clear enough to transcribe. The official 2026 syllabus was not provided. See `NOTES_AUDIT.md` and the “Source material” section in the site for the detailed boundaries.
